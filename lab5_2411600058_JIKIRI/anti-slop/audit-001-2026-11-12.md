# antislop audit 001, 2026-11-12

Project: lab5_2411600058_JIKIRI (Restaurant Inventory System, Laravel + Bootstrap 5)
Mode: AFTER (session override). Nothing below has been changed yet. Pick the numbers to fix.

Design Read: internal restaurant inventory admin for kitchen and storeroom staff, in the existing olive and cream palette from Lab 4 (that palette is the direction, there is no DESIGN.md), dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate and Liveliness = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-25 Contrast.** Amber text on white is below 3:1 whichever Bootstrap amber resolves (about 1.6 to 2:1): the "Low Stock" figure on the dashboard (`text-warning`) and the "Low" label of the stock filter on Inventory (`btn-outline-warning`).
2. **R-03 Mobile.** The sidebar carries `vh-100`. Below 768px it fills a full phone screen before any page content appears. The media query meant to cancel it uses `max-height: auto`, which is invalid and does nothing.
3. **R-03 Tap targets.** Row action buttons (`btn-sm` eye, pencil, trash) are about 31px tall, under the 44px minimum, on Inventory, Categories, Brands and Users.
4. **R-32 Keyboard.** The Category and Brand filters submit the form on every `change`. A keyboard user pressing the arrow keys inside the select reloads the page on each key press.
5. **R-27 States.** Dashboard charts have no empty or error state: if Chart.js fails to load or there are no products, the three chart cards stay blank (`renderCharts` returns silently). The "Recently Updated Products" table also has no empty message.
6. **R-02 Em dash.** Two em dashes in the low stock alert text, `resources/views/dashboard/index.blade.php` lines 56 and 64.
7. **R-38 Sample data shown as real.** Seeded suppliers, brands, prices and quantities look like real vendors and stock, and nothing marks them as sample data. Fix is small (label in the seeder and README, or `[SAMPLE]` marker).

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate and Liveliness)

8. **R-12 Shadow.** Every card type (`.stat-card`, `.chart-card`, `.filter-card`, sidebar, login card) carries a shadow with no written elevation reason, so everything floats equally.
9. **R-19 Animation.** Stat and chart cards lift (`translateY(-5px)`) and grow a shadow on hover, but the cards are not clickable. The motion suggests an action that does not exist. The alert slide-in has no purpose either.
10. **R-06 Typography.** `'Segoe UI', Tahoma, Geneva, Verdana` was not chosen for a stated reason and falls back to different fonts off Windows. Stat labels use uppercase with letter-spacing. Needs your direction (R-37), I will not invent a typeface for you.
11. **R-31 Decorative left stripes.** `.border-accent` on the table header row and a 5px warning stripe on the low stock alert. The alert is already amber, so the stripe repeats it.
12. **R-14 No focal point.** Four identical stat cards in a row: "Total Products" has the same weight as "Low Stock" and "Out of Stock", the two numbers someone must act on.
13. **R-20 No identity motif.** Recolored Bootstrap defaults (cards, striped tables, outline buttons). Swap the name and it could be any Bootstrap admin. Needs direction (R-37): your own `DESIGN.md`, a few answers from you, or a draft from me with the usual warning that agent taste trends generic.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

14. **R-29 Palette.** Olive, dark olive, tan and rust as brand colors, plus Bootstrap success, info (defined, never used), warning and danger. "In stock" green sits close to the brand olive, so status and brand read alike.
15. **R-11 Radius.** No scale: 12px cards, 10px alerts, 8px nav links, 3px highlight, Bootstrap defaults elsewhere.
16. **C-4 Three auth pages still use Tailwind-only Breeze components** (`reset-password`, `confirm-password`, `verify-email`). Tailwind is not loaded, so they render unstyled. Confirmed on `reset-password`, the page behind the emailed reset link.
17. **C-4 Duplicate id.** On the dashboard `#greeting` is on both the `<h2>` and the `<span>` inside it.
18. **C-3 Orphaned files.** `welcome.blade.php`, `dashboard.blade.php` ("You're logged in!"), `layouts/navigation.blade.php`: no route reaches them.
19. **C-1 Dead CSS and JS.** Commented-out `.login-page` and `.login-card` blocks, selectors for ids that no longer exist (`#activityTableBody`, `#toastContainer`, `#inventoryLoading`), two identical `@media` blocks, unused `THEME_COLORS` in `charts.js`.
20. **C-3 Duplicate Logout.** The top navbar and the sidebar both have Logout.
21. **C-4 Charts have no text alternative.** Canvas only, no `aria-label` or data table fallback.

## Checked and not flagged

- R-01 login header gradient (olive to dark olive): stays only if the reason is written down: it separates the brand header from the form.
- R-04 icons: Bootstrap Icons eye, pencil, trash and warning triangle match what they do.
- R-09 badges: In Stock, Low Stock and Out of Stock are real statuses.
- R-10 glass, R-13 glow, R-07 background patterns, R-34 theme toggle: not present.
- R-15, R-16 copy: button labels are specific (Save Product, Add Brand, Create User). No buzzwords, no fake stats or testimonials.
- R-23, R-24, R-26: no invented logo or avatars, and every nav link added in this round has a working page.

## Outside antislop (worth knowing)

- **Public registration is open.** Anyone who can reach the site can create an account and see inventory. With User Management in place you may want to remove the Register link and route.
- **Password reset email.** Your `storage/logs/laravel.log` shows SMTP authentication failures from the Gmail settings, so "Forgot your password?" can error until the mail credentials are fixed (Gmail needs an app password).
